How it works

Seamless from capture to cloud.

Transform clinical dental photography from a manual chore into an automated, compliant workflow.

Step 1

Ingest Daylist & Connect Storage
Connect your practice cloud storage (Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox) under our Bring Your Own Storage (BYOS) model. Upload or snap your daily clinic Daylist—SmileShot’s ephemeral OCR reads patient names and appointment slots in transient RAM with zero permanent storage or AI model training.

Step 2

Capture with Any Camera
Capture clinical photos naturally during appointments using any DSLR, mirrorless camera, intraoral device, or smartphone. SmileShot requires no proprietary hardware—exact capture timestamps are automatically preserved in standard EXIF image metadata.

Step 3

Offline Companion App & Auto-Sorting
The SmileShot Offline Desktop App runs locally on your surgery PC with zero internet requirement. When an SD card is inserted or watch folder updated, it instantly matches photo timestamps to your appointment schedule and structures raw photos into clean Patient Name / Date / Procedure directories with zero lag and zero cloud dependency.

Step 4

Frontend Web Dashboard & Gallery
Access your organized records anytime via the SmileShot Web Platform. Search patients in seconds, browse chronological case timelines, and review high-resolution photo galleries from any surgery workstation or tablet—while original raw files remain safely secured in your own local or cloud storage.
