FAQ

Your questions, answered

Can SmileShot see my patients’ photos?

No. SmileShot only works with folder and file details (names, dates and times) inside one folder in your cloud storage. Our systems can’t open, view or analyse the photos themselves, and our staff can’t either.

Where is my data stored?

Your photos stay in your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. Your account details and the folder information we use are stored on Microsoft Azure in the United Kingdom, encrypted with AES-256.

Do you use my data to train AI?

No. Nothing from your account, your folders or your day lists is ever used to train AI models, ours or anyone else’s.

What happens to a day list photo?

If you use day list capture, the image is read in temporary memory to pick out three things: patient name, date of birth and appointment time. The image is then deleted immediately and is never saved to disk.

What happens if I stop using SmileShot?

Your photos stay exactly where they are, in your own storage. We revoke our access straight away and permanently delete our folder information within 30 days.

Is SmileShot a medical device?

No. SmileShot is an administrative tool for organising photos. It doesn’t analyse images or make clinical decisions, so it isn’t classed as a medical device under UK MHRA rules.

What if there’s a security incident?

We’ll tell you without undue delay, and within 48 hours of confirming any breach that affects your data, with what happened and what we’re doing about it.
