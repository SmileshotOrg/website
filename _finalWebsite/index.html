FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Email us at info@smileshot.ai.

Where are my photos stored?

In your practice’s own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox account. SmileShot doesn’t keep copies of your photos on its servers, so your data stays under your control.

Can SmileShot see my patients’ photos?

No. Your photos go straight to your own cloud storage, and no one at SmileShot can view, scan or check them.

Which cameras does SmileShot work with?

Any camera that saves to an SD card, including Sony, Canon, Nikon, Lumix and Fujifilm mirrorless and DSLR cameras.

What happens if the surgery internet goes down?

Nothing changes for you. The desktop app sorts your photos on the surgery PC straight away and uploads them in the background as soon as you’re back online.

Does SmileShot work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. The desktop app runs on both macOS and Windows, and the mobile app lets you view your photos securely on the go.

Is SmileShot UK GDPR compliant?

Yes. Photos are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256, and we provide a Data Processing Agreement for every practice. Read our DPA.

What happens to my photos if I stop using SmileShot?

They stay exactly where they are, in your practice’s own cloud storage. There’s nothing to export and nothing to lose.

How much does SmileShot cost?

Starter is £15/month, Pro £25/month and Ultra £49/month, with 2 months free when you pay yearly. Our first 50 users get 50% off for life. See pricing.
